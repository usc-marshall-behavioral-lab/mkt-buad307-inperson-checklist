Before You Go
Prepare the day before or morning of
Know your SONA ID — it's the 5- or 6-digit number in your session reminder email. Screenshot it or write it down. You'll need it at check-in.
Confirm your session date and time on SONA. Check the day before — sessions cannot accommodate late arrivals.
Know the location: VPD LL102 — Verna & Peter Dauterive Hall, lower level. 635 Downey Way, Los Angeles, CA 90089. USC Campus Map · Google Maps
Only water is allowed in the lab — no other food or drinks. Eat before you arrive. Sessions last the full scheduled time.
🚪
When You Arrive
Arrive on time — doors close after check-in. Can't find the lab? Text us
Arrive at the scheduled time. You'll enter one by one. A research assistant will open the door and check you in.
Give the RA your SONA ID (5- or 6-digit number) so they can check you off the list. No ID? You can use your name + USC email instead.
Turn your phone completely off (not just silent) and hand it to the RA. It will be placed in a wall pocket and returned at the end.
Place your bag along the back wall. You'll receive a post-it with your ID — stick it on your station.
Sit at the assigned seat — the RA will direct you. Don't choose your own. Friends are seated separately to preserve research quality.
Don't touch the computer until the RA says to begin. Everyone starts together.
💻
During the Session
Follow RA instructions throughout
Complete studies in the order given by the RA — not the order shown in your folder. The RA manages the study rotation for research accuracy.
Raise your hand after finishing each study. An assistant will tell you what to do next.
Read instructions carefully and answer honestly. There are no right or wrong answers. Studies cover marketing topics — surveys, product evaluations, decision scenarios.
If you finish early, stay quiet. You can browse the web on the lab computer while you wait.
You cannot leave early. The session ends at the scheduled time for everyone.
✔
After the Session
Wrap-up and credit
Pick up your phone from the wall pocket on your way out.
Credits are posted by end of day. Check your SONA profile to confirm. If credits don't appear within 24 hours, email mkt.sona@marshall.usc.edu.
Don't discuss study content with classmates or other students. This protects research validity.
⚠
Key Policies
Important rules to understand
One in-person session per semester. A second session earns no credit — the system tracks participation. If unsure whether you've attended, check your SONA credit history.
Late arrivals cannot be admitted. All participants must start together for research validity — no exceptions. Doors close shortly after the session time. Plan to arrive a few minutes early.
Cancel 8+ hours ahead on SONA if you can't make it (illness, emergency, etc.). No-shows or late cancellations count as unexcused and may affect future scheduling.
Your responses are confidential. They are never shared with your instructor or linked to your identity. Your instructor only receives your final credit total at the end of the term.
💡
Tip: No preparation is needed for the studies themselves. Just come on time with your SONA ID, and the research assistants will guide you through everything.
